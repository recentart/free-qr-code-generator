Privacy policy

← Back to the QR code generator

What you type stays on your device

QR codes are created by JavaScript running in your browser. The links, text, email addresses, phone numbers and Wi-Fi details you enter are not sent to our server, and we don’t store them. Downloaded images are created on your device too.

There are no accounts, and we don’t run our own analytics.

Advertising

To pay for itself, this site shows a small banner ad from Adsterra, an advertising network. The ad runs in a separate, sandboxed frame that can’t see this page or anything you type into it.

Adsterra and its advertisers may use cookies and collect technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to show ads and count views and clicks. See Adsterra’s privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
